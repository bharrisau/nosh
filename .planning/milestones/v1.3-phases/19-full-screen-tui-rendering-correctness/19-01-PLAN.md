<objective>
Replace the no-op `?1049h`/`?1049l` flag toggle with a genuine atomic two-grid alternate-screen model in `TerminalState`, resize both grids on SIGWINCH, and gate scrollback so alt-screen content never enters primary history.

Purpose: Full-screen TUI apps (vim, htop, Claude Code) currently render garbled because the alternate screen is a no-op flag — shell text bleeds through and the primary buffer/cursor are lost on exit. This is the foundational correctness fix (TUI-01, TUI-02) and the D-19-09 gate that Phase 22 (scrollback sync) depends on.
Output: `enter_alt_screen()`/`exit_alt_screen()`, a `saved_primary` field, resize of the saved grid, the `!alt_screen` scroll_up gate, RIS reset, and grid-asserting unit tests.
</objective>
